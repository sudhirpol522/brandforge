# 90-second demo script

## 0–15 seconds — Frame the problem

“Creative teams need speed, but brands cannot accept opaque, one-shot generation. BrandForge turns
a brand guide and campaign brief into ranked, editable assets while humans control strategy,
selection, and final approval.”

## 15–30 seconds — Compile the brand

Create the sample campaign. Show extracted colors, fonts, voice, blocked claims, evidence, and the
prompt-injection warning if you added the adversarial line. Approve the structured rules.

## 30–45 seconds — Approve strategy

Show the coordinated Instagram, email, web, and presentation plan. Explain that no expensive image
generation happens until this gate. Approve.

## 45–65 seconds — Compare variants

Show three rendered candidates, six score bars, policy notes, and scorer mode. Explain the funnel:
eight text directions, deterministic rerank, three GPT Images, then OpenAI vision plus diversity
rerank. Select a direction and give a reason code.

## 65–78 seconds — Prove human control

Request a final change once. Show that new variant IDs appear and the old approval is not reused.
Then select and approve the final direction.

## 78–90 seconds — Prove production thinking

Open the export manifest and Grafana view. Mention tenant isolation, model budgets, atomic outbox,
traces, 120 scenarios, 90 tests, no public publishing, and the honest failure-analysis document.
Finish with the real OpenAI evaluation you collected, not the synthetic fixture percentage.
